From the Editors

Beyond the headlines.

The Murshidabad Review is being developed as an independent, research-driven journal for readers who value context, evidence and thoughtful interpretation over the speed of the news cycle.

Named after the historic city of Murshidabad in Bengal, the journal takes inspiration from a rich regional history while looking outward to the world.
